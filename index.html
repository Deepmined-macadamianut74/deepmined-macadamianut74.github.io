🤔 What Is ComfyUI-WorldSculpt?

Imagine taking multiple photos of a cluttered desk — a coffee cup, a book, a lamp — and after running them through WorldSculpt, you get each object as a separate 3D model. You can then rotate them, resize them, and arrange them into your own composition. This software takes the complex technology behind 3D scene reconstruction and packages it with a simple, visual interface using ComfyUI's built-in tools.

Why is it special? Unlike other tools that give you a single merged mesh, WorldSculpt understands individual objects within a scene. It crops out each item from every camera angle, keeps the perspective accurate, and lets you work with them independently. It's powerful, yet it runs entirely on your own computer.

✨ What Can You Do With It?

📸 Load Your Scene

Import a folder of photos along with the camera information. WorldSculpt reads the standard transforms and mask files that many 3D reconstruction tools output, so you can use it right away.

🔍 Isolate Any Object

Select an instance from your scene — say, a chair or a vase — and the tool automatically crops it out from every photo you provided, complete with accurate lens information and 3D positioning.

🧠 Smart AI Integration

Uses the latest AI models (DINOv3, NAF, and the advanced Trellis.2 engine) built directly into ComfyUI. No extra downloads or complicated setup — everything works together seamlessly.

🎛️ Fine-Tuned Control

Adjust the 3D shape stage at a high detail level (grid 64). This replaces the default setting and gives you crisper, more accurate geometry for your objects.

⚡ No Extra Requirements

Everything you need is already inside ComfyUI. This pack adds the WorldSculpt-specific nodes and connects them to the tools you already have. It's genuinely plug-and-play.

🖥️ Works on Windows

Designed to run smoothly on Windows 10 and 11. If you have a standard NVIDIA GPU with at least 8GB VRAM, you're ready to go.

🔧 What Does It Do Behind the Scenes?

You don't need to understand any of this to use the tool, but here's a quick look at the smart pieces inside:

Node Name What It Does For You
WorldSculpt Load Scene Opens a WorldSculpt scene folder and reads all the photo frames plus their mask files (these identify which pixels belong to which object).
WorldSculpt Instance Views Picks out one object of your choice and crops it from every camera angle, keeping the lens distortion correction and placing it in a standard 3D cube boundary.
WorldSculpt LoRA Loader Loads a special lightweight adapter that enhances the 3D model to see the scene the right way — both from a wide view and from the object's perspective.
WorldSculpt Aggregator Loader Loads a smart feature blender that combines the information from multiple photos into one coherent 3D understanding.
WorldSculpt Structure Conditioning Feeds the model with your real camera positions and the fused multi-view features, replacing the default conditioning for a much more accurate result.
WorldSculpt Shape Stage Uses a higher resolution grid (64 vs the standard 32) to sculpt fine details — edges, curves, textures become cleaner and sharper.
